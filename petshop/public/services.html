Five things we do to dogs

Priced by size, because a Cockapoo and a Great Dane are not the same afternoon. Every price below is the whole job — table, bath, dry, finish — with nothing added on at the desk.

The menu

The awkward questions

Asked most weeks, answered the same way every time.

My dog is nervous. Will you still take him?

Usually, yes. Tell us on the booking form under notes, and we will put you on a quiet table with a groomer who has the time. Nervous dogs get shorter sessions and more breaks. What we will not do is wrestle a frightened dog for ninety minutes — if it is not going well we ring you, and you pay for what we managed.

Do you need vaccination papers?

For a first visit we ask that vaccinations are current, because your dog is sharing a room with other people's dogs. Bring the card or photograph it. Puppies can come in two weeks after their second jab.

How often should he be groomed?

Every four to six weeks for a haircut breed like a Poodle or a Shih Tzu, whose hair does not stop growing. Every eight to twelve weeks for most others. Double coats want a de-shed twice a year, and in the two moulting weeks either side of that you will need a brush at home regardless of what we do here.

Can I watch?

At the front, through the window, all you like. Not at the table: dogs settle far faster when their person is not in the room deciding whether to intervene. We text a photo when they are ready.

What if the coat is matted?

We comb it out if the coat allows, and we tell you before we start if it will not. Severely matted fur comes off with a clipper rather than a comb — it is kinder and it is the only way. There is no charge for telling you this, and no charge for saying no and booking you in for a groom you actually want.

Do you groom cats?

No. We are not set up for it and the room is not calm enough. We will happily refer you to a cat groomer in Headingley.

What if I am late?

We hold your slot for fifteen minutes. After that we will fit you in where we can, which may mean a shorter service or another day. The next dog is on the table either way.

How do I pay?

Card or cash when you collect. Peak slots — Saturday and the week before Christmas — ask for a deposit online, and it comes off the final bill.
