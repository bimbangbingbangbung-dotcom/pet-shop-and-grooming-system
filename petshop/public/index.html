How a groom goes

No phone tag, no waiting room, and no surprises at the desk.

  1. Tell us about the dog

    Breed, size, coat, and anything they cannot stand. Two minutes, once.

  2. Pick a day and a slot

    Live availability, two grooms running at once. Booked in about a minute.

  3. We do the work

    Bath, dry, clip, nails, ears. You get a photo when they are ready.

  4. Collect, or stay out

    We text when they are done. The coffee shop two doors down is very good.
